## Description (4,000 chars max)

```
You know the drill. You buy milk because you think you're out. You get home. There are already two cartons in the fridge.

That stops now.

GrubShelf is the app that actually keeps your kitchen organised. Not with complicated spreadsheets or yet another notes app — with a proper system that tracks what you have, what you need, and what you're spending.

HERE'S HOW IT WORKS:

Your pantry, live.
Every item in your kitchen — tracked. Add things by scanning barcodes, searching our catalogue, or typing them in manually. See what's fresh, what's running low, and what's about to expire. All colour-coded so you never have to think twice.

Shopping lists that actually sync.
Create shared lists your whole household can add to. Check things off as you shop. When you're done, transfer everything straight into your pantry inventory. No re-typing. No forgetting.

Scan it. Shelve it.
Point your camera at a barcode and we auto-fill the product details. Name, category, quantity — done in seconds.

Expiry alerts before it's too late.
Get notified before food goes off. Use it, freeze it, or plan a meal around it — but never bin it because you forgot it was there.

One household. One team.
Invite your partner, flatmates, or family. Everyone sees the same pantry, the same lists, the same budget. No more duplicate purchases or crossed wires.

Budget tracking that makes sense.
Set a weekly or monthly grocery budget. Log what you spend at the till. See exactly where your money goes with clear weekly and monthly breakdowns. No finance degree required.

Real impact. Real savings.
Track how much food you've saved from the bin. Watch your waste go down and your savings go up — week by week.

WHY PEOPLE SWITCH TO GRUBSHELF:

- They're tired of buying duplicates
- They're sick of throwing away expired food
- They want one place the whole household can use
- They want to actually see where their grocery money goes

GrubShelf isn't another list app. It's the system that connects your shelf to your shop to your wallet — and keeps all three in check.

Your kitchen, sorted. Download GrubShelf today.
```
