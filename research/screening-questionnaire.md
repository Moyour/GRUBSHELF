# GrubShelf Market Validation & Usability Study: Screening Questionnaire

## Recruitment Goals

* **Primary Segment:** Regular grocery shoppers who experience food waste or disorganization
* **Target Count:** 10 participants
* **Incentive:** $15 gift card (or equivalent) per 30-minute session

## Screening Questions

1. **[Exclusion]** Which of the following best describes your current work?
   - I work for a grocery delivery service, food tech company, or market research firm → **Terminate**
   - I am a professional chef or work in the food/restaurant industry → **Terminate**
   - None of the above → **Continue**

2. **[Behavioral — Frequency]** In a typical month, how many times do you buy groceries (in-store or delivery)?
   - 0 times → **Terminate**
   - 1 time → **Terminate**
   - 2-3 times → **Continue**
   - 4+ times → **Continue** (priority)

3. **[Behavioral — Pain Signal]** In the last 30 days, which of the following have you experienced? (Select all that apply)
   - Threw away food that expired before I could use it → **Continue** (strong signal)
   - Bought something at the store and realized I already had it at home → **Continue** (strong signal)
   - Forgot to buy something I needed because I didn't have a list → **Continue**
   - Went over my intended grocery budget → **Continue**
   - None of the above → **Terminate**

4. **[Articulacy Check]** Think about the last time you went grocery shopping. Walk me through how you decided what to buy and how the trip went.
   - *Look for a storied response with specific details (checked the fridge, made a list on phone, forgot X, realized Y at checkout). One-word or generic answers ("it was fine") signal a poor fit for a qualitative interview.*
