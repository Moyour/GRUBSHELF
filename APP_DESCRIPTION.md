## Full description

### Stop buying what you already have

7 out of 10 people say they regularly buy groceries they already have at home. GrubShelf fixes that by connecting your shopping list directly to your pantry. When you try to add something you already own, the app tells you — with quantity and when it was last updated.

### Know what's expiring before it's too late

See at a glance which items need to be used soon. GrubShelf tracks expiry dates, nudges you before food goes bad, and helps you plan meals around what you already have. Less waste, less guilt, more of the food you actually love.

### Your pantry builds itself

Start with a shopping list. Check items off as you shop. When you're done, transfer everything to your pantry in one tap — quantities, categories, and expiry dates included. No manual data entry. Your inventory stays accurate without the work.

### Your whole household on the same page

Create a household and invite family members, partners, or roommates. Everyone sees the same pantry and shopping lists in real time. No more "did you get the milk?" texts. No more duplicate purchases.

### Budget tracking that actually works

Set a weekly or monthly grocery budget. Log trip costs when you transfer items to the pantry. See how you're tracking at a glance from the dashboard — spending trends, savings from avoiding duplicates, and waste impact.

### Smart shopping lists

- Multiple lists per household
- Catalog search with smart suggestions
- Duplicate warnings when something is already in your pantry
- Quantity steppers on every item
- Mark all complete, then transfer to pantry
- Home Screen widget to check items off without opening the app

### Everything else you'd expect

- Barcode scanning (Open Food Facts)
- Expiry calendar view
- Low stock alerts and usage reminders
- Data export (JSON & CSV)
- Sign in with Apple, Google, or email
- Light and dark mode
- Native iOS design, fast and private

## Privacy

GrubShelf does not sell your data. Your pantry and shopping information is stored securely and shared only within your household. See our full privacy policy in the app under Settings > Privacy Policy.

## Features

### Authentication & account security

- Sign in with Apple
- Sign in with Google
- Email sign-up and sign-in
- Email verification flow
- Forgot password (6-digit email code)
- Session restore on launch

### Onboarding & first-run experience

- Story-driven product walkthrough
- Household creation (required before main app)
- Post-onboarding setup: create a shopping list, invite members, set a budget
- Guided cold-start experience

### Dashboard

- Contextual hero card: expiring items, low stock, or shopping list prompt
- Quick actions: Add Item, Shopping, Log Purchase
- At-a-glance grid: pantry items, expiring soon, budget, waste events
- Shopping progress with completion percentage
- Story bubble strip: shopping, budget, low stock, waste, streak
- Household activity feed

### Pantry

- Filters: All, Expiring, Low Stock, Categories
- Search by name
- Add Item Hub: barcode scan, catalog search, manual form
- Item lifecycle states: Active, Low Stock, Expiring Soon, Expired, Stale, Archived
- Swipe actions: edit, delete
- Removal flow: Used vs Wasted (optional waste cost)
- Auto-archive of expired items with configurable grace period
- Pantry review flow for stale items

### Shopping lists

- Multiple lists per household
- Inline add with grocery catalog suggestions
- Duplicate purchase warnings with pantry quantity and time context
- Confirmation dialog before adding items already in pantry
- Per-item completion checkboxes with quantity steppers
- Complete All pending items
- Transfer to Pantry with editable quantities, optional expiry, trip cost
- Home Screen widget support

### Insights

- Weekly or monthly budget with multi-currency support
- Spending progress and trends
- Waste impact tracking
- Log Purchase: amount, date, store
- Analytics cards: spending trends, duplicate savings, activity metrics

### Profile & household

- Family members with roles (owner, admin, member, guest)
- Invite members via email or in-app
- Approval workflow for non-admin additions
- Data export (pantry and shopping data)
- Sign out and account deletion

### Settings

- Appearance: System, Light, Dark
- Pantry: auto-archive toggle, grace period
- Notifications: expiry, low stock, usage review reminders
- Help: FAQ, About, Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy

### Home Screen widget

- Shopping list snapshot on Home Screen
- Shows pending items and counts
- Mark items done from the widget

### Platform

- Native SwiftUI iOS app (iOS 17+)
- Supabase backend (PostgreSQL, Auth, Realtime)
- Sign in with Apple, Google, Email
- Open Food Facts barcode integration
